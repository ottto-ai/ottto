negative certificate. A clean upload follow-up remains unsettled even when the
native observations completed. Real synthetic transcripts and paired evidence

persist a different family witness. It reports whether the actual writer kept

the synthetic index. A baseline failure is diagnostic evidence, not a policy fix

changes. No installed behavior, historical incident attribution, identity-row
acceptance or resource saving is established by this fixture. Production repair
and any per-family recovery carrier require separate review and disposition.

# Scoped Claude unhealthy retries

Same-context unhealthy retries retain complete families with an exact accepted upload-body head and unchanged committed scan identity. Eligibility does not require OTEL proof, request-census proof, a closed duplicate fence, a future replay deadline, or census completion. A changed file is parsed normally; after its new body is accepted, it is eligible on the next retry. Carry credits no current-window proof and changes no bindings, ACKs or identity latch.

Invalid pending stays on BASE except a narrow id-less case. A bounded revision marker records an singleton observation missing request IDs; any other member, revision, parse loss, decode uncertainty or invalid cause refuses the exception. The marker is never proof, and missing or damaged older provenance refuses carry. Pending remains invalid, the fence remains closed and withheld identity remains withheld. Parser or scan-identity changes require a fresh read. Protected families and account/authority holds remain excluded.

A due RetryPending deadline wakes only a traversal started before that deadline, so a deleted or decode-held family cannot repeatedly bypass hourly backoff. Same-context restarts at exhausted red terminals discard process-local carry decisions and recompute from durable state at the next due start. Mid-walk, Fix 2 window, missing-provenance and context-change starts retain BASE. Carry/cancel buffers use the existing bounded scratch and replay funding. Local logs count carried and re-armed families without adding a wire field.

Validation includes the unchanged independent pipeline acceptance test, no-OTEL accepted families, changed/reaccepted eligibility, id-less evidence preservation, second-cause refusal, changed fingerprints, version changes, restart and third-cycle backoff, protection, scratch refusal and late identity emission.

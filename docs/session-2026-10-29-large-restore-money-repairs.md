# Large-record restore money repairs — 2026-10-29

Warm, cold and unsampled publication routes preserve the frozen floor. The durable route freezes the accepted Claude entry before candidate
mutation. A shorter extent or a newer accepted body revision holds without an
upload, unread-byte acknowledgment or unreadable-source classification. The
record is checked before decode and admission, then the actual prepared extent
is checked before native body I/O. Unknown identity keeps the floor. Only a
strictly bound original with independent device/inode and birth disagreement can
identify replacement; that exit is counted separately and starts a fresh Full.

An interrupted retail grant remains spent. Exhausted and unavailable accounting
on real growth can instead acquire one recovery Full per source/key per six
wall-clock hours. One bounded map owns this recovery pace, separate from the
spend ledger. Backwards time and implausible jumps expire toward reading. At
capacity the oldest stamp is evicted, with a separately counted overflow alarm.
Both maps reserve their worst-case nodes from the existing cache allowance; the
cache total does not grow. The pace is statically bounded below one day.

Growth beyond the committed size bypasses every existing metadata and opened-FD
age filter. Shorter extents and future accepted revisions also retain their
entries so age pruning cannot erase S2. This is discovery, not recovery authorization: the
refusing owner must also grant paced recovery. Held attempts retain their
accepted entry and committed census evidence, and grant no settlement credit.
Missing records retain the integrated counted-Miss behavior rather than the
removed UpgradeMissing hold. Every refused Codex group member is recorded before
the group exits; no short-circuit hides later refusals.

Native fixtures cover growth, zero-read paced holding, wall-clock expiry,
cumulative cold-Full equality and an idle restart. A warm same-inode truncation
control verifies zero body reads/publication and preserved accepted witness. Clock rollback, large forward
jumps, map overflow, frozen accepted facts and independent replacement proof
have separate controls. Existing spent-custody hold controls now explicitly set
up an interrupted recovery attempt so their zero-read assertions remain bounded
by the recovery pace. These fixtures are compiled by Clippy; tests are CI-only.
The receipt retry fixture explicitly requires the qualified macOS heap layout.

Capture-only incompatibility remains removed. Source/counting incompatibility
still returns the exact original before a fresh Full; transient preparation
failures retain the existing return/custody protocol and three-attempt bound.
